About Us

Entrepreneurship, innovation, technology, quality and long-term customer relationships.

NR Group of Companies is a diversified business group focused on building reliable, customer-centric businesses across multiple sectors.

Our journey is driven by entrepreneurship, innovation, technology, quality and long-term customer relationships. We aim to create and develop businesses that provide practical solutions, generate value for customers, and contribute to sustainable growth.

Under the NR Group umbrella, we are building a portfolio of businesses and services across areas such as Roadside Assistance, Travel & Tourism, Business Technology, Digital Services, Automotive Services and other emerging business opportunities.

We believe every successful business starts with a simple idea — identify a need, create a useful solution, and serve customers with commitment.

Our Vision

To build NR Group into a trusted and diversified business group with a strong presence across multiple industries.

Our Mission

To create customer-focused businesses powered by quality, technology, innovation and responsible business practices, while continuously exploring new opportunities for growth.
